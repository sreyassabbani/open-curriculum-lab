The 4.3 boundary illustrates how to use parallel sources. Its videos teach first partials, second and mixed partials, and briefly demonstrate implicit differentiation. OpenStax 4.3 supplies geometric/contour-map interpretation, the continuity condition for equality of mixed partials, and a PDE introduction. OpenStax 4.4 develops differentiability; OpenStax 4.5 develops the chain rule and implicit differentiation. Thus a 4.3 lesson can point forward to 4.4 and 4.5 while reserving their full methods and checks for those lessons.
