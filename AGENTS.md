# Writing

Understand the content and the reader's purpose before choosing its format.
Use connected prose for explanations, lists for distinct items or ordered
actions, and tables when readers need to compare the same attributes. Add
headings where they help navigation. Let the material determine the structure;
avoid filling a template with unnecessary bullets, labels, callouts, or repeated
summaries. Use simple, concrete language. Preserve any genuinely required
course or technical format while applying this judgment within it.
